Privacy Notice

How we handle the details you send us.

What we collect

When you use our contact form we collect your name, email address, phone number (if you give one), the service you're interested in, how you'd like us to contact you, and your message.

When you use the “Tell us about your business” assistant on our Services page, we store the description you type and the suggestion it gives, without your name or contact details.

Why we collect it

Only to reply to your enquiry and to understand which of our services businesses need most. We don't sell your details or use them for unrelated marketing.

Who can see it

Our team, through a password-protected admin area. Messages are sent to us by email (Google Gmail), the website is hosted by PythonAnywhere, and the assistant's suggestions are generated by Google's Gemini service.

How long we keep it

For as long as we need it to deal with your enquiry and any work that follows, after which we delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you. Email info@picglobalsolutions.com and we'll respond promptly.
